# Strategy Building Research

Research date: 2026-09-05. These are reference patterns from specified games, not a survey of their latest balance. Our proposed mechanics are distinguished from source observations.

## Battlezone 98 Redux

The manual describes a Recycler that deploys at a geyser and produces initial support and combat units. A Constructor places buildings, some requiring power. The Mobile Unit Factory produces advanced vehicles; the Armoury supplies the battlefield. Units can be recycled into scrap. This connects field resources, production facilities, and the commander's direct battlefield activity. Source: [Battlezone 98 Redux manual](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/steam/apps/301650/manuals/BZ98R_Manual_GB.pdf). The full PDF fetch exceeded the research tool's size limit; these observations come from its indexed manual passages.

**Our adaptation:** A bootstrap headquarters, physical collection, dedicated building and vehicle production functions, and salvage that returns value from combat. Cards authorize new commitments; support structures perform the work.

## Civilization VI

The official manual describes districts spreading a city's buildings across terrain and receiving placement-dependent adjacency benefits. This makes city layout an economic decision. Source: [Civilization VI manual](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/steam/apps/289070/manuals/CIV_VI_25TH_ONLINE_MANUAL_ENG.pdf), indexed “Districts and Buildings” section.

**Our adaptation:** Deposits and sources determine extractor placement; route length, service reach, and defensibility determine base layout. Initially express benefits through actual shorter deliveries and connected services rather than arbitrary bonuses for two neighboring buildings.

## Age of Wonders 4

The developers describe separate building and unit queues, using Production and Draft respectively, so army replacement need not stop city development. Province improvements depend on terrain, while boosts link city structures to developed provinces. Town halls unlock further structures, and top-level guild choices encourage specialization. Source: [Cities and Expansion developer diary](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/age-of-wonders-4/news/age-of-wonders-4-dev-diary-8), March 9, 2023.

**Our adaptation:** Separate constructor work from factory work, but let them compete for the same physical materials. Give advanced structures functional prerequisites and let rare buildings specialize an established economy.

## Proposed shared structure

The synthesis is: establish a foothold, collect materials, deliver them, expand capacity, manufacture and sustain forces, secure more territory, and specialize. [[Core Resources]] and [[Common Base Buildings]] translate that structure into this game's rules. [[Rare Building - Coupler Foundry]] explores one exceptional branch.

We are not importing food, gold, mana, draft, or science as additional stockpiled currencies. Power, work rate, storage capacity, and research progress can be tracked properties of actual facilities and operations.
