# Common Vehicle Weapons

[[Units and Equipment Index|Units and Equipment]]

## Standard fits

**Assault:** One top-mounted cannon plus one missile rack. The cannon is a direct-fire projectile weapon for deliberate armour hits; the rack delivers a limited burst against a selected target before reloading. The top turret turns independently of chassis direction within its mechanical limits. Missiles require their own target solution and can miss, be obstructed, or lose tracking.

**Scout:** One top-mounted machine gun plus a spotting/designation mast. Its gun suits harassment and exposed equipment, while the mast supports observation, Survey Sector, and designation for a separately authorized Airstrike.

These two fits are the basic roster. Alternatives below are common weapon design candidates for the first expanded catalogue, not already balanced replacements.

## Assault main-turret alternatives

| Weapon | Distinct use | Cost or weakness | World interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard cannon | Accurate single armour hits at medium range | Reload interval and projectile travel | Peek from terrain; target exposed turrets or modules |
| Twin autocannon | Sustained tracking against Scouts and light equipment | Lower armour penetration; heat under prolonged fire | Disrupt an exposed Worker tool or convoy escort |
| Scatter cannon | Close-range burst across a broad area | Weak at range; commitment to a close approach | Defend a loading lane or punish a rushed flank |
| Lobbed mortar | Indirect pressure on a marked position | Minimum range, slow shells, poor moving-target accuracy | Fire over a ridge using Scout observation; terrain still blocks unsuitable arcs |
| Charged pulse cannon | Deliberate high-impact energy shot | Visible charge, heat, and a vulnerable firing interval | Force a defender to move or expose its firing window |

Each replaces the standard main gun rather than adding a second main turret. The Legendary Gravitic Lance also occupies this slot when fitted; its unique-capability rules remain in [[Rare Building - Coupler Foundry]].

## Assault rack alternatives

| Rack | Distinct use | Cost or weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard guided missiles | Pursue a tracked vehicle after acquiring a solution | Limited volley; tracking can break behind terrain |
| Unguided rocket pod | Saturate a known short-range area | Poor precision; dangerous near friendly units and cargo |
| Air-defense missiles | Protect Workers from visible aircraft | Restricted target class and useful firing envelope |
| Smoke launcher | Break optical sightlines and cover a retreat | Replaces damaging missiles; active sensors may still detect through smoke according to their mode |

Smoke is a defensive rack replacement, not a universal free ability. Mortar and rocket explosions damage only under explicit blast/cover rules; they do not automatically delete every nearby connection.

## Scout alternatives

| Slot | Equipment | Distinct use | Cost or weakness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light turret | Standard machine gun | Mobile harassment and defense against light targets | Weak against intact Assault armour |
| Light turret | Burst autocannon | Short accurate attacks on exposed modules | Longer recovery between bursts |
| Light turret | Needle rifle | Precise long-range shots on small visible equipment | Low total damage and demanding aim |
| Light turret | Light grenade launcher | Lob pressure into cover or around loading areas | Slow projectile and weak moving-target tracking |
| Recon | Standard designator | Mark a target for available precision air support | Requires maintained observation to keep the solution current |
| Recon | Wide-area scanner | Detect nearby signatures and locate hidden resource opportunities | Replaces precision designation; active scans reveal the Scout's emission to eligible sensors |

A sensor return is not automatically a precision weapon lock. Scout alternatives should preserve reconnaissance value without making it a smaller Assault with identical jobs.

## Spotting and airstrike sequence

1. The Scout observes a target and starts designation. Initial tuning: 2 seconds of stable tracking establishes a solution.
2. A valid mark provides targeting data to an available friendly strike asset. The player's Airstrike card still pays its normal cost and commits that aircraft's actual approach.
3. Continued observation updates a moving target. Breaking sight retains a last-known location for 3 seconds, clearly marked stale, then removes precision tracking.
4. An aircraft with a still-valid solution can attack. A point strike can use the last-known location if the player explicitly accepts it; the target may have moved.

Destroying or suppressing the Scout before weapon release can degrade the attack. A released munition follows its declared tracking rules, not an automatic recall. Possessing a Scout does not grant an aircraft, Airstrike card, or knowledge through terrain.

## Ammunition and energy baseline

Weapon ammunition, magazine size, heat, and capacitor charge are tracked unit properties, not new universal resources. Initial military production includes a full standard load. Physical rearming at a Service Bay replenishes ballistic/missile stores using Metal; exact per-round prices and capacities are deferred to combat tuning. Energy weapons recharge from onboard systems, limited by charge time and heat.

Workers do not burn the base Generator's Wood to move; onboard propulsion is independent of base industrial power. Ammunition rearming and ordinary repairs are free service orders with material consumption. Manufacturing or fitting a new weapon requires the existing refit authorization and physical equipment/recipe. Removed intact weapons become stored equipment, not free refunds and duplicated guns.

Friendly fire, component hitboxes, minimum ranges, charge times, ammo counts, and damage must be exercised in actual combat before the alternatives are promoted to tuned baseline equipment. Support clear component targeting without requiring the player to hit a tiny connector on every encounter.

## First comparison to try

Start with the standard Assault cannon/rack and Scout machine gun/designator. Add Twin Autocannon and Smoke Launcher first to compare sustained light-target fire with deliberate cannon shots and defensive retreat. Keep the other alternatives as designed candidates until those roles are distinguishable in real play.
